# Cross-paper Synthesis

Inputs: reading notes, research question, previous synthesis. Produce a new version under artifacts/05-outline/ each round using [the template](../templates/synthesis.md).

1. Group by question or dispute, selecting meaningful comparative dimensions. Do not organize the manuscript primarily by author or paper.
2. Check task definitions, data, metrics, sample conditions, or mathematical assumptions before comparison. Do not rank results from incompatible settings; explicitly state when direct comparison is unsupported.
3. Record agreement, disagreement, possible explanations, and unresolved questions, each linked to DOI and note location. For inferred explanations state what evidence is still needed.
4. Distinguish “not found”, “existing but weak evidence”, and demonstrated limitations when describing gaps. Search absence is not proof of no research. Record search boundaries and contrary evidence.
5. Update weak_dimensions with specific questions, search directions, and completion criteria. Prioritize core questions over citation counts.

Acceptance: each outline section has a question, proposed claim, support/opposition, and limits. Cross-paper conclusions compare multiple papers; single-paper claims identify that scope. Collect further evidence or report gaps when core support is insufficient.

Assess scope and counts separately: production remains 40 core and 20 expansion papers. If coverage appears saturated below target, present evidence and the shortfall for the user's decision. If counts pass but support does not, collect more or narrow claims. One round without new coverage is a signal, not an automatic quota waiver.
